# LDgenX: image enhancement brief

These 14 images are **real photographs and documents published by LDgenX**. They are on the new website now, at the best resolution their CMS keeps (1000 px at most, often less). The goal is a **cleaner, sharper version of the same picture**, not a better-looking version of the subject.

The client's rule: visitors must see **the equipment, places and work as they really are**. If a pump, filter, sampler or lab bench is changed, even "improved", a customer could receive equipment that doesn't match the website. That breaks the client's trust.

## Rules for every image

1. **Allowed:** upscaling (×2, or the target size given below), sharpening, removing JPEG blocks and noise, gentle exposure and white-balance correction, straightening a horizon by at most 1–2°.
2. **Not allowed:**
   - adding, removing, moving or reshaping any object;
   - changing the shape, colour or proportions of equipment;
   - "cleaning" scratches, cables, stickers or wear on the equipment;
   - changing a person's face, body or clothes;
   - replacing a background;
   - inventing detail that isn't there (no hallucinated screws, labels or textures).
3. **Text, logos, handwriting, credits and numbers** stay exactly as they are, letter for letter (e.g. "SEQUENCH / PATENT PENDING", "0.45 micron", "@biotope", "Témoin a / Témoin b", map legends). If a piece of text cannot be sharpened without guessing letters, leave it soft rather than retyping it.
4. Keep the **framing and aspect ratio**. No crop, no extension of the canvas (no outpainting).
5. Keep the photo looking like a **photo**: no HDR glow, no beauty filter, no added depth of field.
6. **Delivery:** PNG (or JPEG quality ≥ 92), same file name with `-enhanced` added, e.g. `02-pompe-triple-interieur-enhanced.png`. One file per source; do not merge images.
7. If an image cannot be improved without breaking these rules, say so and return it unchanged.

## Do not send these to GPT

- **Scientific figures** (Leitwein et al., Saint-Pé et al., Delpuech et al., Aliakbari et al.) and the **Artois-Picardie report figures**. They are data: an AI upscale can change a number, a point or a bar. The site now serves the largest published versions (700–1000 px). For sharper versions, ask the authors for the original files (PDF/SVG/TIFF).
- **Team portraits.** Studio portraits vs original photos is the client's decision. The original photos are in `images/official/equipe-*.jpg`.
- **Partner logos.** They are already cropped from the published files; ask the partners for vector logos if needed.
- **Species photos on case cards** (sea urchin, shrimp, white seabream, shark, lapwing, sea bass). They are stock or third-party photos, already 1000 px and sharp.
