# Useful Calendar records — 29 October 2026

The previous exports held the correct times but displayed generic unavailability and repeated whole-team blocks across three calendars. The administration now exposes useful records instead of bare blocked slots.

- Google events carry the appointment format, staff, status, duration and direct LDgenX record link. Blocked times use their actual title by default. Client names/organisations and appointment subjects remain private unless an administrator explicitly enables sharing. Meeting links, event language and popup appointment reminders are configurable. No attendees, invitations or email reminders are added.
- Whole-team blocks use one “LDgenX · Équipe” calendar by default. Personal appointments/blocks stay in the relevant individual calendar. The administrator can choose individual copies instead. Changing mode or person removes obsolete managed copies. The owner should select the team calendar in Google’s sidebar to see common blocks. An unrelated older Maeva calendar is preserved.
- A block opens a detailed drawer with people, exact dates/times, Paris time zone and actual Google export state/view links. Administrators can edit its title, person, multi-day range and all-day mode, with inclusive last-day input. All-day Google dates retain their exclusive end boundary, including daylight-saving transitions. Version checks reject stale edits instead of overwriting another administrator’s change. Deletion requires confirmation.
- Google-origin busy periods open read-only details and a Google source link. Private external calendars remain FreeBusy-only. Identical busy spans applying to every person display once, while staff filters still work. Dedicated work-calendar titles are retained. LDgenX-managed records remain authoritative in the portal; Google links lead back to the exact record/date, including through login.
- Booking protection retains live server availability checks, buffers, database locks and exclusion constraints. The durable queue is written with the reservation, then exported after commit. Queued retries run every minute; complete horizon reconciliation is throttled to five minutes. Per-item acknowledgement retains exact PostgreSQL revision text. A committed reservation is not reported as failed because Google is temporarily unavailable.

Two additive migrations were applied and the Calendar and portal Edge functions deployed. Team-calendar creation uses the existing grant. A durable creation claim avoids duplicate calendars after an ambiguous response, with a restricted advanced recovery option. A team-provisioning problem leaves whole-team work queued while allowing individual appointment exports to continue.

Validation includes 28 Calendar/portal unit and network-mock tests and 16 actual connected-Google assertions: create/move/cancel, useful metadata, access restrictions, team consolidation, reassignment, all-day dates, stale-edit rejection, per-item acknowledgement/view links, duplicate-free inbound overlays and complete removal of QA copies. Disposable accounts and events are removed. Fourteen browser flows pass and cover login destination, settings, FR/EN, mobile layout, detail/edit/create/delete, deep links, month/list views and unavailable-Google feedback; connected interface mutations use synthetic fixtures after checking the real connection status.

Production build, TypeScript, Deno checks, lint and typography pass, together with 151 static HTML pages and 147 indexable pages (zero SEO errors or warnings). Visual review also corrected the new checkbox layout and displays full years and inclusive multi-day duration in block details. No OVH email, mail DNS, SMTP, Cloudflare email setting or paid subscription is changed. Staff calendar sharing/mapping and Google Cloud publishing/verification remain account configuration responsibilities; `primary` currently refers to the connected owner for each person. Independent changes in Google cannot be made atomic with a portal database transaction.
