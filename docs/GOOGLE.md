## Calendar integration

The owner chose dedicated LDgenX output calendars and inbound Google busy intervals. The owner authorized the connection on 29 October 2026; real appointment and blocked-time creation/deletion have now been verified against Google. The intended owner account is `admin.ldgenx@gmail.com`. Each bookable staff member gets a separate app-created calendar. Calendar access is a distinct administrator-only connection, not a permission requested during ordinary sign-in.

Open `/admin/parametres?onglet=google`, connect the owner account and grant both Calendar permissions. The callback is `https://www.ldgenx.fr/api/auth/google/callback`, already listed in the supplied web client. The Pages worker forwards this callback to Supabase; it never receives a client secret. Single-use hashed OAuth states expire after ten minutes. The callback rechecks the initiating active administrator, verifies the Google account’s verified email, checks the granted scopes and requires offline credentials. A short connection lease prevents duplicate simultaneous provisioning.

Scopes are `openid`, `email`, `calendar.app.created` (create/manage only app-created calendars), and `calendar.events.freebusy` (busy intervals from accessible calendars). External calendars are queried through FreeBusy, without event titles/descriptions. Only ID, times, transparency, status and LDgenX markers are read from dedicated output calendars. Exported appointments use generic status labels, no client identity or project subject, no attendees and no invitations.

Map every staff calendar that should block bookings to that staff member. Every listed calendar must be shared with the connected Google account, with at least free/busy permission. `primary` means the connected owner’s main calendar and is initially included for each staff member as a conservative default. Replace or supplement that default with each person’s actual calendars. App-created output calendars are already checked and must not be entered as external busy sources. Each person may have up to 15 external calendar IDs; the union is capped at Google’s 50-calendar FreeBusy limit. Unshared or unlisted calendars cannot be checked.

Availability and every booking/move/confirmation are checked against Google on the server immediately before reservation. Existing Postgres booking locks and exclusion constraints remain in place. Google intervals include the configured booking buffer. Failures of an enabled connection reject new bookings/moves with a clear localized error. Requested appointments and both spans of pending changes are held. Portal appointments remain authoritative: edit/cancel them in LDgenX. Manually added output-calendar events block that person; manually moved/deleted managed events are reconciled to the portal state, with unexpected occupied times treated as busy until repair.

Appointment and blocked-time changes enqueue exports in the same database transaction as the reservation. Exports run after commit and retry every five minutes with exponential backoff through a private durable outbox. IDs are idempotent, including deterministic replacements for Google tombstones. Outbox revisions are read and compared as exact PostgreSQL text so the driver cannot round microseconds during acknowledgement. Blocked times have their own private queue and mappings; whole-team blocks produce one Google event per bookable staff member. Matching managed block copies are excluded from incoming Google busy overlays, and deletion removes every managed copy. Reconciliation checks the upcoming booking horizon (up to 93 days per scan). Week/month/list views use Paris time and display Google busy intervals without deletion controls. A Google failure never reverses an already-committed booking or reports it as unsaved.

Credentials use AES-256-GCM encryption before storage in the private schema. The encryption key, Google client credentials and job key are server secrets; ignored local provisioning copies retain mode 0600. OAuth tickets, mappings and outbox are private with RLS and no browser grants. Disconnect removes stored credentials and leaves protection enabled after an established connection, suspending bookings until reconnection. It retains existing calendars/events and does not revoke basic Google sign-in. Google access can also be revoked through the owner’s Google account settings.

Provisioning: `node scripts/setup-calendar.mjs` (additive migration, server secrets, conditional cron), (including both Calendar migrations), then `node scripts/deploy-calendar.mjs` and `node scripts/deploy-supabase.mjs`. Never regenerate an existing encryption key unless deliberately reauthorizing the connection. No SMTP, OVH or DNS configuration is involved.
