## Services

[**Environmental DNA (eDNA)**](https://www.ldgenx.com/services/environmental-dna-edna)

Detection and monitoring of species through the analysis of DNA traces present in the environment.

[**Population genomics**](https://www.ldgenx.com/services/population-genomics)

Assessment of genetic diversity, population connectivity, and local adaptation.

[**Microbial Community Analysis**](https://www.ldgenx.com/services/microbial-community-analysis)

Characterization of bacterial, fungal, and microbial communities using high-throughput sequencing technologies.

[**Genomic Selection**](https://www.ldgenx.com/services/genomic-selection)

Development of genetic tools to support breeding and selection programs.

[**Bioinformatics**](https://www.ldgenx.com/services/bioinformatics)

Analysis, processing, and interpretation of genomic data.

[**Sequencing & dPCR**](https://www.ldgenx.com/services/sequencing-dpcr)

Amplicon sequencing, short- and long-read sequencing, and development and analysis of digital PCR (dPCR) assays
