# Our partners

LDgenX collaborates with research **institutes**, **universities**, **environmental consultancies** and **companies** to develop **innovative** scientific solutions.

These partnerships bring together complementary expertise to address complex challenges in biodiversity **conservation**, **agriculture**, and **aquaculture**.

## Public Research Institutes

Our expertise is rooted in years of experience within leading public research institutes, where we received our scientific training and carried out our first research projects.
