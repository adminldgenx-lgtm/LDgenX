# Equipment

## Triple pump

- Three separate parallel filters with*Waterra* capsules

- Inlet hose size: 12 mm or 4 mm diameter

- Maximum height between the water surface and the pump:

1.5 m with 12 mm diameter hoses and 5 m with 4 mm diameter hose (model 1)
3 m with 12 mm diameter hoses and 15 m with 6 mm diameter hose (model 2)

- Effective pumping speed: 3 L/min (model 1) or 10 L/min (model 2)

- Three integrated flow meters

- 8-hour battery life (model 1) or 10-hour battery life (model 2) with included Opti Battery lithium-ion battery

- Pore size: 0.1 µm, 0.22 µm, 0.45 µm, 1.2 µm, and 5 µm
