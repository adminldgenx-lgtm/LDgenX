Sequencing & dPCR

LDgenX supports projects requiring sequencing technologies, digital PCR (dPCR), and bioinformatics analyses, delivering tailored solutions from experimental design to data interpretation.

Approaches

Amplicon Sequencing

16S, ITS, COI, 12S, 18S and custom genetic markers.

Metagenomics

Taxonomic and functional analysis of biological communities.

Whole-Genome Sequencing (WGS)

Comprehensive genome analysis.

RAD-seq & Custom SNP Panels

Population genomics and genotyping applications.

Long-Read Sequencing

Oxford Nanopore sequencing for long-read genomic analyses.

Digital PCR (dPCR)

Ultra-sensitive detection and quantification of target species, pathogens, or genetic variants.
