## Characterize the microbial diversity of your samples

## Microbial Community Analysis

LDgenX supports projects investigating bacterial, fungal and other microbial communities using high-throughput sequencing technologies to characterize their diversity, composition, and functional potential.

## Approaches

**16S Sequencing**
Analysis of bacterial community diversity and composition.

**ITS Sequencing**

Characterization of fungal communities.

**Microbial Metabarcoding**

Comprehensive assessment of microbial diversity using targeted genetic markers.

**Metagenomics**

In-depth taxonomic and functional analysis of microbial communities through untargeted sequencing
