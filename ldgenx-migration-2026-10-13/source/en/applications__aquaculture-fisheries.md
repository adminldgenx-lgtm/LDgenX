# Aquaculture & Fisheries

### ***Optimizing the Management and Performance of Aquatic Resources***

Genomic technologies provide new **opportunities** to **improve** the **management** of exploited populations, optimize breeding programs and enhance the **sustainability of aquaculture production.**

LDgenX supports **producers**, **research** organizations and **fisheries managers** in developing **innovative** genomic **solutions** for aquaculture and fisheries.

Health surveillance
