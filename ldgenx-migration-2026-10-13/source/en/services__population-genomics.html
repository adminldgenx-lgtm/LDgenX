Understanding genetic diversity to better manage and conserve populations

Population Genomics

Population genomics enables the characterization of genetic diversity, population connectivity, and adaptive potential to support effective conservation and management strategies.

LDgenX develops tailored genomic approaches to address challenges in biodiversity conservation, population management, and scientific research.

Approaches

Reduced-Representation Genome Sequencing (3D-GBS)

Genotyping thousands of genome-wide markers to assess genetic diversity, population structure, and connectivity.

Whole-Genome Sequencing (WGS)

Comprehensive genome analysis for variant discovery, adaptive genomics, and evolutionary studies.

Custom SNP Panels

Development of tailored SNP panels for genetic monitoring, individual assignment, parentage testing, and conservation programs.

Genetic Marker Discovery

Identification of SNPs, indels, and other genetic variants tailored to the objectives of your project.
