About LDgenX

Genomics for Biodiversity, Agriculture & Aquaculture

LDgenX is a DeepTech company specializing in the application of genomics to biodiversity conservation, sustainable agriculture, and aquaculture.

We support public and private organizations in their research, environmental monitoring, and innovation projects through solutions combining environmental DNA (eDNA), population genomics, high-throughput sequencing, digital PCR (dPCR), and bioinformatics.
 

Our ambition is to make genomic technologies accessible and actionable, empowering better decision-making to address today's and tomorrow's environmental challenges.

Our Expertise

With over 10 years of experience in genomics and bioinformatics, the LDgenX team provides expertise across the entire project lifecycle:

Scientific project design
Sampling strategy
Molecular analyses
Genetic tool development
Sequencing and digital PCR (dPCR)
Bioinformatics analyses
Data interpretation and reporting
 

We develop tailored solutions to meet the specific needs of each project, delivering robust, reliable and actionable results.

Our Mission

We believe that genomic technologies are a powerful tool to improve our understanding, management and conservation of biodiversity.

Our mission is to support environmental managers, researchers, public authorities, consulting firms and industry partners in harnessing these technologies to generate reliable data that inform evidence-based decision-making.

An Innovative DeepTech Company

Innovation is at the core of LDgenX's development.

We conduct research and development to design the next generation of genomic tools for biodiversity monitoring and the sustainable management of natural resources.
 

Our innovation projects are supported by leading funding programs and institutional partners, including:

  • DeepTech Label
  • French Tech Emergence Grant
  • Créalia
  • Bpifrance
  • University Innovation Hub (Pôle Universitaire d'Innovation)
  • Partner research institutes and universities
