# Environment & Consultancies

### ***Innovative Tools for Environmental Monitoring and Assessment***

LDgenX supports environmental **consultancies**, public **authorities** and **renewable** energy **stakeholders** in their environmental **monitoring projects** using environmental DNA (eDNA), metabarcoding and molecular biology technologies.

Our approaches provide **robust**, **non-invasive** data to **improve** ecosystem knowledge and support informed decision-making.

Environmental impact assessments
Baseline environmental surveys
Biodiversity monitoring
Long-term environmental monitoring
Detection of protected species
Detection of invasive species

Land development projects
Onshore and offshore renewable energy projects
Offshore wind farms
