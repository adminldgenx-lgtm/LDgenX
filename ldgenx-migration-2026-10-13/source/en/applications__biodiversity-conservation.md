# Biodiversity & Conservation

### ***Genomic Tools for Biodiversity Conservation***

As biodiversity continues to decline, **conservation managers**, **public authorities** and **research** organizations need **reliable tools** to monitor populations and support i**nformed management decisions**.

LDgenX supports conservation projects with next-generation genomic technologies, providing robust data to guide biodiversity **monitoring** and **management**.

## Applications

Monitoring of threatened and protected species
National Action Plans (NAPs)
Assessment of genetic diversity
Detection of inbreeding
Hybridization studies
Ecological connectivity analyses
Individual assignment and origin tracing
Detection and monitoring of invasive species
Reintroduction and ecological restoration programs
