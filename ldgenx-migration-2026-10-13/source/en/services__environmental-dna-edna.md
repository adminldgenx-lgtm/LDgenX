## Monitor biodiversity using environmental DNA (eDNA)

## Environmental DNA (eDNA)

Environmental DNA (eDNA) enables the detection and identification of species present in an ecosystem from water, soil, sediment, or biofilm samples.

LDgenX provides tailored eDNA solutions to support biodiversity monitoring, conservation, and environmental impact assessments.

## Approches

**Metabarcoding**
Biodiversity assessment using targeted genetic markers (e.g. 12S, COI, 18S, ITS).

**Metagenomics**
Comprehensive analysis of biological communities through untargeted sequencing.

**Targeted dPCR**
Ultra-sensitive detection and quantification of target species or pathogens using digital PCR (dPCR).

**Long-Read Sequencing**
Enhanced taxonomic resolution and expanded analytical capabilities through long-read sequencing technologies.
