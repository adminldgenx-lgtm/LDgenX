## Developing the Genomic Tools of Tomorrow to Better Understand and Protect Biodiversity

## *Innovation at the Core of LDgenX*

We develop **innovative** solutions combining **environmental DNA**(eDNA), **next-generation sequencing**, **artificial intelligence** and **bioinformatics** to make biodiversity monitoring more accessible and more effective.

Our goal is to transform genetic data into **decision-support tools** for stakeholders in environmental management, agriculture and aquaculture.

## Innovative Projects

### DNA Ecotrace

DNA EcoTrace is an innovative project developed by LDgenX to automate and simplify biodiversity monitoring using environmental DNA (eDNA).

The project combines:

- Non-invasive sampling

- Long-read sequencing

- Artificial intelligence

- Automated data analysis

- User-friendly reporting

Its objective is to provide environmental managers, public authorities, environmental consultancies and companies with reliable information on ecosystem health to support informed decision-making

### eDNAClock

eDNAClock is an innovative project aimed at estimating the age of fish from traces of environmental DNA.

By combining environmental DNA (eDNA), epigenetics and next-generation sequencing, the project explores the potential of **epigenetic clocks** to characterize the demographic structure of aquatic populations.

**Applications:**

- Fisheries management

- Species conservation

- Aquatic ecosystem monitoring
